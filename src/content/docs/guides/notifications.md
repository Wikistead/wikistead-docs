title: Notifications & watches
---

You do not follow everything — you **watch** what matters and Wikistead tells you when it moves.

## Watching

The bell (🔔) on a page toggles a watch:

- **Watch a page** — you are notified when it changes.
- **Watch a space** — the same, for anything in the space.

Your watches are listed (and managed) in one place: **Watches** in the sidebar. Unwatch from there or from the page itself.

## What arrives, and where

Changes to watched pages reach you as notifications — in the app's notification bell, and by email where your deployment has email configured (self-hosted instances bring their own SMTP; see the self-hosting guide).

## Watching vs. the changes feed

Watches are your *push* channel for the pages you care about; the [recent changes feed](/guides/recent-changes/) is the *pull* view over everything you can see. Skim the feed daily, watch the pages weekly.
