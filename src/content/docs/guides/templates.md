---
title: Templates
---

A template is a page you write once and stamp many times — meeting notes, ADRs, runbooks, weekly plans.

## Using templates

When creating a page, pick a template instead of starting blank: the new page opens pre-filled with the template's content, ready to edit. The template gallery lives at **Templates** in the sidebar, where you browse everything the workspace has.

## Making templates

A template is authored like any page — the full editor, macros included. Anything a page can hold, a template can pre-fill: a `:::todo` checklist, a skeleton table, the tag frontmatter your convention expects.

## Knowledge-first, unlimited

Templates are a knowledge primitive, not an upsell: they are unlimited on every plan. Teams that agree on shape agree faster on content — encode the shape once.
