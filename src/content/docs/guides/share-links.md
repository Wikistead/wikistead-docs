---
title: Share links
---

A share link is Wikistead's front door for everyone who is not a member: open the link and you are reading — or commenting, or editing, live — **without an account**. The link *is* the onboarding.

## Creating one

On a page, open **⋯ → Share** and create a link. Choose what it grants:

- **Capability** — *view*, *comment* or *edit*. One link carries exactly one capability.
- **Scope** — this page, or (where enabled) a whole space, for the anonymous-wiki pattern where anyone with the link can edit any page in the space.
- **Expiry** — optionally give the link a lifetime; an expired link stops working on its own.

## What guests are

Guests arrive with a caret, a colour and a guest label, and type alongside members in real time. Deliberately, guests are **not** members:

- They do not appear in your member list and never consume a billable seat.
- They hold nothing durable — their entire access *is* the link.
- They are permission-checked like everyone else: an edit link to one page is not a key to the space.

## Revocation

Revoking a link is **one action with immediate effect**: every guest who entered through it loses access at that moment. Nothing to clean up, no accounts to disable — because guests never had accounts.

## Security posture

Links are unguessable capability URLs. Anyone holding one has what it grants — treat an edit link like the key it is, scope links narrowly, set expiry for anything you post somewhere semi-public, and revoke freely (you can always mint another).
