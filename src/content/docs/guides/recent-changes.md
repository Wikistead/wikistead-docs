**Changes** in the sidebar is the workspace's pulse: a reverse-chronological feed of page activity across everything *you* are allowed to see.

## What it shows

Each entry is a page event — created, published, updated — with who and when. Follow an entry to the page, and to its [revision history](/guides/pages/#revisions) when you need the exact diff between versions.

## Permission-shaped, per reader

The feed is filtered by the reader's own access: a page you cannot view does not appear in your feed — not even as a title. Two members with different access see two different feeds, correctly.

## The wiki habit

Recent changes is the classic wiki ritual for a reason: skimming it once a day is the cheapest way to know what the team learned yesterday. For targeted following instead of a firehose, [watch](/guides/notifications/) the pages you care about.
