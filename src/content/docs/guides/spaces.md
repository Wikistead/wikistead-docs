A space is a page tree with a front door: one team, project or topic, its own home page, its own access.

## The shape

- The **space switcher** at the top of the sidebar moves between spaces and creates new ones.
- Each space has a **home page** (the space's landing page) and a **page tree** below it.
- The tree scales: drag to nest and reorder, expand and collapse branches; a page's place in the tree is part of the knowledge, not just storage.

## Access

Who can enter a space is decided in the space's settings — membership grants, per-page access on top, and (if enabled) public exposure are documented in [Space settings](/guides/space-settings/), [Share links](/guides/share-links/) and [Public spaces](/publishing/public-spaces/). The rule of thumb: a space is the unit you grant, a page is the unit you fine-tune.

## Many spaces, one knowledge model

Personal notes, the team wiki and the public docs are all just spaces — the same pages, the same editor, the same links between them. Moving knowledge from "mine" to "ours" to "everyone's" is a move between spaces or a publishing setting, never a migration to a different tool.
