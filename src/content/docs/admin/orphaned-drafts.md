Drafts are visible to their creator only — so when a member leaves, their unpublished drafts would otherwise be invisible to everyone forever. **Admin → Orphaned drafts** (admins) is the recovery surface for exactly that case: it lists drafts whose creator is no longer active in the workspace, so an admin can rescue the work instead of losing it.

This tab does not open *active* members' drafts: while the creator is in the workspace, their drafts stay theirs alone. It exists only for drafts whose author has left.

## Rescuing a draft

Two steps, in order:

- **Claim** grants the admin temporary read access to the draft, so its content can be reviewed before deciding what to do with it.
- **Reassign** hands the draft to a chosen member — its new, permanent owner — which also ends the admin's temporary access immediately. An unclaimed-but-unreassigned access also expires on its own, so a claim can never turn into standing access by being forgotten.
