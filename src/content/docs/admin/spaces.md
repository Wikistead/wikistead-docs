---
title: Spaces (admin)
---

**Admin → Spaces** (admins) lists every space in the workspace — including ones you are not a member of — for workspace-level administration: create spaces, see who manages what, and step in when a space has lost its manager.

This is the tenant-wide view; day-to-day space administration (members, pages, trash, moderation of *one* space) lives in that space's own [settings](/guides/space-settings/). The division: space managers run their spaces, this tab exists so the workspace can always see and recover the whole estate.
