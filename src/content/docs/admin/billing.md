---
title: Billing
---

**Admin → Billing** (admins) is where a Cloud workspace sees its commercial state: the current **plan**, **seat** usage against the plan's limit, and **storage** consumption.

Three principles shape everything on this tab:

- **Guests are never billed.** Seats count members; people arriving over [share links](/guides/share-links/) cost nothing, however many collaborate.
- **Downgrades are non-destructive.** Going over a lower plan's limits blocks *new* growth (new invites, new uploads) — it never removes members or deletes content. The exact per-lever downgrade behaviour is in the [entitlement levers reference](/reference/generated/entitlement-levers/).
- **Self-hosted Community has no billing to do** — every lever resolves unlimited; this tab concerns the managed Cloud.
